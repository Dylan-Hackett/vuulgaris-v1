# 0006 - 2.42" SSD1309 over SPI for the display

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** pre-2026-08-05 (recorded retroactively from design state section 7)

## Decision

**2.42" SSD1309, 128x64, SPI.** LCSC **C5139768** (HS242L01W4S01), $12.22 qty 1.
Active area 55.01 x 27.49mm, supply 1.65-3.3V.

## Why

- **Only display family with a shipped libDaisy driver.** `OledDisplay` uses the SSD130x
  driver, and libDaisy issue #166 confirms SSD1309 in SPI mode. No ST7789 / ILI9341 /
  SSD1322 driver exists, so anything else means writing your own transport, which is
  exactly where audio glitches come from.
- **1KB per full frame.** SSD1322 (256x64, 4-bit grey) is 8KB. A 320x240 TFT at 16bpp is
  150KB, which is 150x the bus traffic and 150x the window competing with audio and CV timing.
- 3.3V supply matches Patch SM's 3V3 rail directly. No level shifting.
- No backlight, better off-angle legibility than TFT.
- 2.5x the diagonal of a 0.96" panel at the same pixel count.

Interference ranking, worst to best: **I2C anything -> TFT over SPI -> SSD1322 -> SSD1309.**

## Consequences

- These are **bare panels with an FPC tail**, not breakout boards. The board needs an FPC
  connector, not a 2.54mm header. Check pin count and pitch against the panel datasheet.
- SSD1309 panels usually need external charge-pump caps and an IREF resistor.
- DC and RST are **two GPIO not counted in early pin estimates**. Some modules tie RST to a
  pullup, saving one.
- DMA buffers must be in the DMA memory section and at **global scope**.

## Stock warning

**27 units at LCSC.** The second SPI option C5139769 has 8. I2C versions are better stocked
(C7466000 blue 67, C7466001 white 22) but I2C is the interference-worst choice, so that is
not a real alternative.

2.42" SSD1309 SPI is a commodity format widely available outside LCSC. **Plan on
second-sourcing for production.** Cheaper fallback if needed: **C5139767**, 1.54" SPI,
49 in stock, $7.21@1 / $5.06@100, which is also cost lever #2.

## Open items

- **[unverified]** Whether MISO (D8) is truly free. Depends on the panel being write-only,
  usually yes for SSD130x. Confirm.
- **[unverified]** libDaisy SPI DMA is documented as non-blocking and IRQ handlers exist
  for SPI2-SPI5. `OneBitGraphicsDisplay::Update()` returns true when finished, described as
  being for chained DMA transfers. But the `SendDataDma` line in SSD130x source appears
  commented out, so the shipped `Update()` may still be blocking. At 1KB it does not matter
  much either way.
- **Test early:** a 2022 report on Daisy Patch showed audio working but the OLED dead under
  both `BOOT_QSPI` and `BOOT_SRAM`. May be fixed. Since this design has both a display and
  the bootloader, verify that path before it is load-bearing.

## Hard rule

**Keep display refreshes off the audio callback.** CV output timing is documented as
degradable by OLED and MIDI activity. No full-frame redraws during timing-sensitive CV.
